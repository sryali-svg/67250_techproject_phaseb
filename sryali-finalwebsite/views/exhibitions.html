Jackson Pollock

Jackson Pollock helped redefine modern painting through his energetic drip technique and large-scale abstract compositions. His work emphasized movement, spontaneity, and gesture, transforming the canvas into a record of physical action.

Helen Frankenthaler

Helen Frankenthaler became known for her soak-stain method, which allowed color to flow directly into unprimed canvas. Her work bridged Abstract Expressionism and Color Field painting, creating luminous compositions that feel both expansive and intimate.

Willem de Kooning

Willem de Kooning’s paintings combined abstraction with the human figure, producing works that feel intense, layered, and constantly in motion. His approach to form and brushwork challenged clear boundaries between representation and abstraction.
