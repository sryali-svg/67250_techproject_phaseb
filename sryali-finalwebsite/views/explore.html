"The abstraction is often the most definite form for the intangible thing in myself that I can clarify in paint." — Georgia O'Keeffe

MonoMuse is a museum dedicated to connecting its visitors to moments in artistic history. By focusing on different artistic movements in each of our seasons, MonoMuse hopes to brings its users to the instants in time that transformed the face of the artistic world as we know it today. With our current focus, Abstract Expressionism, MonoMuse hopes to connect users with transformational ideas of movement, deconstruction, texture, and emotion.

MonoMuse’s doors are open to all. We welcome students, families, artists in all forms, researchers, and members of our community here in Pittsburgh and beyond. With its constantly shifting theme, MonoMuse hopes to bring its visitors back to a place of artistic exploration again and again.

In 2016, Monomuse was founded as a place to connect with independent artistic movements, in a more immersive way. By focusing on a single artistic movement at a time, Monomuse has always sought to create a focused experience that allows visitors to resonate with the influences, techniques, and unique perspectives that defined these movements.
