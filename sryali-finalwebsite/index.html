Welcome to MonoMuse!

MonoMuse is a contemporary museum dedicated to exploring one artistic movement at a time. Our focus on Abstract Expressionism allows visitors to experience the artists, techniques, and ideas that transformed painting into an act of movement, emotion, and experimentation.

MonoMuse is a contemporary museum dedicated to exploring one artistic movement at a time. Presently, the museum focuses on Abstract Expressionism, a movement that redefined art through gesture, emotion, spontaneity, and scale. Our exhibitions center on artists essential to shaping the Abstract Expressionism movement, such as Jackson Pollock, Helen Frankenthaler, and Willem de Kooning. MonoMuse invites visitors to explore how painters used abstraction, color, and movement to challenge traditional ideas of representation and push the boundaries of the artistic world forward. By focusing on a single movement, MonoMuse creates a more immersive experience that helps visitors understand the shared influences, techniques, and cultural context that shaped some of the most important works of twentieth-century art.
